# Decision Science & Analytics Portfolio 📊

> *"Data tells you what happened; Decision Science tells you what to do about it."*

This repository is a collection of experiments applying Machine Learning, Optimization, and Decision Theory to real-world problems, with a specific focus on **Auto Lending** and Financial Modeling.

- `numpy` & `pandas`: The bedrock of data manipulation.
- `scikit-learn` & `xgboost`: For predictive modeling.
- `matplotlib` & `seaborn`: For visualizing decisions.
- `scipy` & `networkx`: For optimization and policy mapping.
