## 🗓️ Release 5: Causal Inference (Difference-in-Differences)
**Visual**: `causal_impact_plot.png`
**Text**:
> "Correlation is not Causation. But we can measure the difference." 🔬📉
> 
> If we lower our minimum FICO requirements in State Y, and default rates go up... was it our policy? Or was the whole economy just doing badly?
> 
> This project applies **Causal Inference (Difference-in-Differences)**. We use a control state to calculate a "Counterfactual" (What would have happened without the policy change). By comparing the actual default rate to the counterfactual, we isolate the pure causal impact of the decision.
> 
> #CausalInference #DataAnalytics #DecisionScience #Econometrics
